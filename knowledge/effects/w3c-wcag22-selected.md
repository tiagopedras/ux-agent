# WCAG 2.2, selected success criteria

Source: <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>. Pulled 21 Sep 2026. W3C Recommendation, 12 December 2024.

Partial copy: only the criteria reviews have needed so far, in the W3C's wording. Add more
through `ux-add-reference` as reviews need them. Understanding pages are linked per row.

| SC | Name | Level | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1.1 | [Non-text Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-content.html) | A | "All non-text content that is presented to the user has a text alternative that serves the equivalent purpose", with listed exceptions. |
| 1.4.3 | [Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | AA | "The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1", except large text, incidental text and logotypes. |
| 2.1.1 | [Keyboard](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/keyboard.html) | A | "All functionality of the content is operable through a keyboard interface without requiring specific timings for individual keystrokes", with an exception for path-dependent input. |
| 2.2.1 | [Timing Adjustable](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html) | A | "For each time limit that is set by the content, at least one of the following is true:" Turn off; Adjust ("over a wide range that is at least ten times the length of the default setting"); Extend ("warned before time expires and given at least 20 seconds to extend the time limit with a simple action"); Real-time Exception; Essential Exception; 20 Hour Exception. |
| 2.2.4 | [Interruptions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/interruptions.html) | AAA | "Interruptions can be postponed or suppressed by the user, except interruptions involving an emergency." |
| 2.3.3 | [Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) | AAA | "Motion animation triggered by interaction can be disabled, unless the animation is essential to the functionality or the information being conveyed." |
| 4.1.3 | [Status Messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) | AA | "In content implemented using markup languages, status messages can be programmatically determined through role or properties such that they can be presented to the user by assistive technologies without receiving focus." |
