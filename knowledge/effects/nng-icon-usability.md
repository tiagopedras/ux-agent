# Icon Usability

Source: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/icon-usability/>. Pulled 21 Sep 2026. Published 27 Jul 2014.

By Aurora Harley, Nielsen Norman Group.

## "Universal" icons are rare

Home, print and the magnifying glass for search have mostly universal recognition. "Outside
of these examples, most icons continue to be ambiguous to users."

## Icons need a text label

"A text label must be present alongside an icon to clarify its meaning in that particular
context."

"Icon labels should be visible at all times, without any interaction from the user."

## The 5-second rule

"If it takes you more than 5 seconds to think of an appropriate icon for something, it is
unlikely that an icon can effectively communicate that meaning."

## Why it matters

Icons that do not communicate their meaning become "confusing, frustrating, eye candy — and
to visual noise."
